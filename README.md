# The Final Drive

A two minute drill game you play in the browser. One drive a day: you trail by 1 to 8 points with 1:20 to 2:40 left, starting between your own 15 and 45 with zero to three timeouts.

You call every snap. Short pass, sideline pass, deep pass or run; a timeout or a spike when the clock is running; a field goal when you are down 1 or 2 and inside their 35. The clock only moves when you call a play.

Each result is one real NFL play, drawn for your down, distance and field position from 19,963 plays and 2,970 sacks (last seven minutes of a half, 2016 to 2025, nflverse play-by-play, CC BY 4.0). The teams are made up.

Every call is graded before you see what happened, so an unlucky good call still scores well. Same drive for everyone each UTC day; send the link and compare coach scores with a friend.

Free. No account needed. No ads.
